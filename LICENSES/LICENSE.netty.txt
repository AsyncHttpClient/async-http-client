This product contains a modified copy of Netty's DefaultHttp2LocalFlowController,
adapted from Netty 4.2.17.Final, in:

    client/src/main/java/org/asynchttpclient/netty/channel/
        SuspensionAwareHttp2LocalFlowController.java

The adaptation makes connection-window auto-refill switchable at runtime so it
can be scoped to the lifetime of a suspended HTTP/2 response. Netty's own
implementation fixes that mode at construction time and keeps its state private.
